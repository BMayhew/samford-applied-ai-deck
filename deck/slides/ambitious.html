Be more ambitious

"I frequently proclaim (together with other people working on AI) that we ought to be more ambitious when using it."

Felix Rieseberg, How to be more ambitious: A tutorial for humans with models

Ask the model: "What's the goal beyond the goal?"

be-ambitious

A skill built from Felix's article. It turns your task into a bigger plan and a more demanding prompt.

robonuggets/be-ambitious

Applied AI with Claude | Samford University

Felix works on Claude at Anthropic. His point: most of us ask these models for far less than they can do. He breaks ambition into speed, altitude, depth, and range. Altitude is the one to try first: describe the outcome you want, then ask what should happen after that. Run /be-ambitious on your next idea and compare the plan to what you would have asked for.
